## Section 4 — The model never judges its own actions

**Addendum, 15 September 2026.** The keyword router described above is now **off**. Re-measured
on 15 September, narrowing the tool list gave 0 points of tool-choice gain on gemma4:12b — and it
had already been without effect in the engine since 5 September. What replaced it is the opposite
choice: a *stable* prompt with all 21 tools, every time. Two reasons, both measured. The twelve
real quick-bar phrases hold at 100 % with the full prompt and the full tool list
[code-verified: scripts/eval-results/gemma4_12b-barre-1509-2026-09-15T10-40.json]; and a prompt
whose prefix never changes is *faster* than a shorter one that changes shape, because Ollama
caches the prefix: 1.1 s warm against 4–6 s on every switch between the bar and the panel
[doc-only]. The classifier still runs, but it only sets the thinking level and the attachment
rule. The lesson replaces the earlier one: on a 12B model with tool definitions really
transmitted, *fewer tools visible* was not what made tool choice reliable — the definitions
were.
Source: docs/kora-orchestrateur.md « Fusion Kora/agent — achevée le 15/09 », commit c166c91.

## Section 5 — Three risk tiers, two journals, one confirmation

**Addendum, 15 September 2026.** "One Kora": the lightweight quick-command path
(`quick-action.ts`, its own prompt, five tools, no persona, no memory) is deleted. Everything a
closed vocabulary does not recognise in the command bar now goes through the single agent loop,
on a *profile* that says only what belongs to the path — the six reversible actions without a
bubble at the keyboard, `control_media` alone by voice, a 45 s cap, thinking `low`, no history,
never a cloud escalation for "turn it down". The tier table did not change. What is new on the
security side: write, delete, memory and delegation tools are now reachable from the bar and
from the voice, with their normal confirmation bubble — red for the irreversible — answerable by
voice. The "not from here" refusal is gone; the confirmation is what remains
[code-verified: src/renderer/orchestrator/quick-command-profile.ts ; quick-action.ts no longer
exists]. Not yet exercised at the real microphone [doc-only].
Source: docs/kora-securite.md (note of 15/09), docs/persistance.md « Une seule Kora », commit c166c91.

## Section 8 — Voice, entirely local

**Addendum, 15 September 2026.** The wall moved. A *guided* capture screen (⚙ → Listening →
Advanced) tells the user which situation to record next — forty utterances crossing distance,
voice, posture and noise, a third of them with music or television on — and records on the
space bar. Forty-three new "Kora" in one afternoon brought the user's own recordings to 127.
A candidate trained on them (207 s, CPU, the GPU model untouched), measured at the service's
own threshold on the two frozen sets: misses on the first set 12/29 → **9/29**, alignment
0.534 → 0.690, median peak 0.905 → 0.959; the never-seen set unchanged at 2/20; false wakes
4.5 → 4.0 per pass [code-verified: scripts/eval-results/wakeword-candidate-1509.json]. Three of
the four promotion conditions pass; the fourth — false wakes per hour of *shadow* listening in
the real room — is pending, so the candidate is not promoted. First measured gain of the whole
wake-word effort, and it came from exactly where section 8 said it would: more timbres of the
user's own voice. Rule confirmed: the friction was not recording, it was remembering to vary.
Source: docs/apprentissage-mot-d-eveil.md « Deuxième mesure (15/09) », commits b7a6384, 8337c9c.

## Section 10 — Capture for learning from day one

**Addendum, 15 September 2026.** The "acts" pile is no longer empty. Three sources now fill it
without a gesture. (1) The rate bench records: `eval:agent-rates --runs 3 --record` on the 27
cases gave 150 successful passes (93 %; the six failures all on "put the music back on"), from
which `prepare.py` keeps 80 tagged `bench` examples and freezes five *measure* cases that are
never trained on — three of the five are cases where the first fine-tune had regressed
[doc-only]. (2) The command bar and the voice path log their tool turns too (`tool_turn_log`,
source `barre` or `voix`), with confirmations as labels and a thumbs-up/down under the status
line for turns that have no bubble — the bar was the largest lost source of real actions
[code-verified: src/renderer/ui/tool-turn-recorder.ts]. (3) The guessing game writes preference
pairs (one per turn: the warmer guess is "chosen"); the first real game, "avion" found in four
tries, put three pairs in the log [doc-only]. Caveat noted in the doc: 22 bench prompts repeated
twice are not 80 independent examples — the second fine-tune will only be probative once the
real tool turns outnumber them.
Source: docs/fine-tune-premiere-tentative.md (15/09, « Le banc comme source »), docs/persistance.md, commits 4309ab8, c166c91.
